The Lost Kingdom

Long ago, a peaceful kingdom was protected by an ancient crystal.

But one day, the Shadow King stole its power and disappeared into a forgotten castle.

Monsters now roam the forests, darkness covers the land, and the kingdom is slowly falling apart.

You are the last hero capable of reaching the castle.

Your journey begins now...

📜 The Story

Long ago, the Lost Kingdom was protected by an ancient crystal. The crystal brought peace and power to the kingdom.

But one day, the Shadow King appeared and took control of the ancient castle.

Monsters now roam the forests, and the kingdom is slowly falling into darkness.

⚔️ You are the hero chosen to restore the kingdom.
